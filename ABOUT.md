## What Makes It Stand Out

### 🧠 A prediction engine that respects reality
Most apps slap FPL's `ep_next` on a card and call it a day. We go further:

- **Market-first projections** — when odds providers are wired in, every player's expected points are rebuilt from bookmaker goal/assist/clean-sheet probabilities per fixture.
- **DGW-aware everywhere** — double gameweeks are summed correctly across fixtures, not quietly clipped like in other tools.
- **Team strength differentials** — a City forward vs Luton isn't priced the same as a Burnley striker vs Arsenal, and the model knows it.
- **Form, momentum & hot streaks** — player form, team form, and performance-vs-expectation compound into the projection.
- **Penalty takers, minutes probability, fixture congestion** — all baked in, not bolted on.

### 📰 AI News Signals (powered by Gemini)
The app scrapes trusted FPL sources — YouTube transcripts, RSS feeds, Twitter — and uses **Gemini 2.0 Flash** to extract per-player signals: *starts, benched, injured, returning, hot form, rotation risk, set-piece changes*.

Those signals then **adjust the expected points** of every affected player, automatically. You get the edge of someone who watches every press conference, without watching a single one.

### 🤖 AI Team Rating & Transfer Advisor
Paste your FPL team ID. Get:

- A brutally honest **AI rating of your squad**, weak links flagged.
- **Transfer recommendations** tailored to your bank, free transfers, and upcoming fixtures.
- **Chip advice** for the current gameweek — which chip to play, accounting for what you've already burned.

### ⚡ Auto-Select Best XI (and Team of the Week)
- One click picks the **highest-scoring XI from your squad** for any of the next 9 gameweeks — formation, captain, bench order, done.
- **Team of the Week** shows the best XI from the *entire player pool* so you know who to target in transfers.

### 📊 Live Gameweek, Done Right
- Real-time points with **tie-aware provisional bonus** (handles ties *and* double gameweeks — most live trackers don't).
- Mini-league live tracking.
- No double-counting when official bonus lands.

### 🎯 Player Finder That Actually Finds
Price slider, team/name search, availability filters, fixture difficulty, expected points, form, ownership — sorted the way *you* think about FPL, not the way a database thinks.

### 📱 Beautiful on Mobile
Built mobile-first with drag-and-drop pitch, swipe-friendly player sheets, and animations that feel like a native app — because most FPL decisions happen on the bus, not at a desk.
